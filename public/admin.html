Monster attacks

Send monsters against a city or a village. While it lasts, a city's bridges are raised, the monsters gather round the town and fight anyone there, players close by get a warning across their screen, and players in the towns round about hear the news while the townsfolk gather in the plaza to talk about it. It ends when its time runs out, or when you drive them off.
